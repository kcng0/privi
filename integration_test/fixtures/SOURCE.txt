Synthetic playback fixtures generated for Privi tests; no personal or third-party media.
Generated with FFmpeg 6.1.1 using scripts/create-playback-fixtures.sh.
Inputs are FFmpeg testsrc2/sine generators and the subtitle text in that script.
Redistributed with this project under its MIT license. These files are never app assets.

Regenerate locally:
  bash scripts/create-playback-fixtures.sh integration_test/fixtures
  (cd integration_test/fixtures && sha256sum *.mp4 *.mkv *.webm *.ass > SHA256SUMS)

The checked-in corpus avoids installing a video toolchain during every emulator run.
CI verifies SHA256SUMS before serving the corpus to the app's private test cache.
